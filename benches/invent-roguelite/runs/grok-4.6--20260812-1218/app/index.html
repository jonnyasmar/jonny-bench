How to Play

You are a Pilot-Spark

A remnant will that once steered a star. You have no blade. You gather relics into orbit and let the sky do the killing.

Syzygy

When two relics line up through your core, a killing axis ignites. Pulse in that window for a Nova. Fling in that window for a Spear of Heaven. Reverse orbits to force the alignment.

The run

Three Heavens. Chambers, elites, altars, sovereigns. After each fight an Edict rewrites your physics. Die, and you keep Ash for the Reliquary.
